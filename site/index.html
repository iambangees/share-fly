How it works, honestly: both devices open this page at the same time and connect with a code. Files travel directly browser-to-browser over WebRTC — no file ever touches a server, and nothing is stored anywhere. A free signalling service (PeerJS cloud) only introduces the two browsers. Keep both tabs open until the transfer finishes; the page asks your screen to stay awake while sending or receiving. Very large files work, but they take a while — the progress % is per file. On a strict office/school network the direct connection can be blocked; on home Wi-Fi and mobile data it normally just works. Works on PC, Android and iPhone, all in the browser.
